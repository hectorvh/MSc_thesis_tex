\section{Glossary}
Agent: an agent denotes an autonomous or semi-autonomous entity capable of perceiving its environment and acting within it. 

Situational Awarness (SA): perception of elements in time and space, comprehension of their meaning, projection of their near-future status (3 levels). 

Workspace awareness: Gutwin and Greenberg (2002), they define  as the up-to-the-moment understanding of how other people are interacting with a shared workspace. Workspace awareness is a specialised form of situational awareness. 

Measured with SAGAT(freeze the sim, ask queries), SPAM (real-time probes, no freeze) orSART (self-rating). 

Team SA: each member has the SA their role needs 

Shared SA =the overlap on requirements they share. 

CRDT (Conflict-free Replicated Data Type) is a data structure for keeping shared state in sync across several computers without a central server deciding who wins. 

CSCW (Computer-Supported Cooperative Work) is the research field that studies how people work together through software, and how to design that software. It sits between computer science, HCI and social science 

Ad-hoc: made for this specific case, not derived from a general method or theory. 

CCTV stands for closed-circuit television and is a video surveillance system that transmits signals to a specific, limited set of monitors rather than broadcasting them publicly.

Agent: an autonomous or semi-autonomous entity capable of perceiving its environment and acting within it

HRI human–robot interaction: the research field that studies how people and robots communicate, cooperate and share control. It sits between robotics, human factors, HCI and cognitive psychology
